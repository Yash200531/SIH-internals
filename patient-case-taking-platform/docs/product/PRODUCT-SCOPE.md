# Product Scope

## Primary outcomes

- Reduce history-taking and documentation burden.
- Improve completeness and continuity without fabricating clinical information.
- Help patients understand and confirm what was captured.
- Make longitudinal records usable through consented, source-linked summaries.

## Initial users

- Patient and caregiver.
- Reception/assisted operator.
- Nurse and doctor.
- Facility operations and privacy teams.

## MVP exclusions

- Autonomous diagnosis or prescribing.
- Replacement of a hospital's complete HIS/EMR.
- Mandatory ABHA enrollment.
- Direct handling of diagnostic imaging pixels as a PACS replacement.
- Training foundation models on identifiable production records.
